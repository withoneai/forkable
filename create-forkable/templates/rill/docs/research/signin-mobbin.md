## Five patterns, ranked for your sign-in

1. **Superhuman card, two actions.** Centered card, product name, one tagline, "Sign in with One" as the filled primary, "Try the sample inbox" as a ghost button directly under it. Rise and Chronicle prove two actions is enough. Best fit because your screen has exactly two choices.
2. **Blurred live inbox behind the card.** Superhuman's sign-up modal and practice inbox both sit on the real inbox, blurred. Render your sample inbox behind the card. The "try the sample" button then literally unblurs it. No illustration needed, works in dark mode for free.
3. **Split with AI output on the right.** Clay, Unify, and Fireflies put a real product artifact on the right, cropped off the edge. For you: a sample thread with the AI summary and a drafted reply, 55 percent of the width, dark panel.
4. **Permission explainer and trust line.** Fireflies' "Why does this need access to my calendar" link plus encryption line, and Pin's three benefit rows. Put one sentence under the One button: "Connects Gmail or Outlook. Read-only until you send." Add a small trust row at the bottom.
5. **Last-used and progressive disclosure.** Linear highlights the method used last time, Vercel collapses extras. On return visits, promote One and move the sample inbox to a text link.

## Typography and spacing

The best screens use a 15 to 16px system sans (Inter or SF) at 400, a 20 to 24px semibold title, and a 13 to 14px grey subtitle, with a form column of 320 to 360px. Buttons are 36 to 40px tall, full width, stacked at 8 to 12px gaps, with a 24px gap before the email fallback. Dark screens use near-black (#0A0A0A to #121212), inputs one step lighter, and a single accent colour on the primary button only. Serif titles (Unify, Tines) read as premium but only on dark or warm backgrounds.
