# Skill: launch-checklist

**Reads:** everything else in `gtm/`.
**Output:** `gtm/out/launch-checklist.md`.

Produce a checklist covering: domain bought and pointed at the deploy,
`forkable deploy` run, the Connect app's scopes match
`connect.manifest.json`, a privacy page exists (`PRIVACY.md` or a `/privacy`
route), analytics wired (if any), and a first-10-users plan drafted from
`gtm/out/creator-outreach.md` and `gtm/out/launch-post.md`.

Draft only. This is a checklist for a human to work through, not something
this skill executes on its own.
