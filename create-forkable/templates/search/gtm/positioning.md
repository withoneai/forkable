# Positioning

Filled in by `gtm/skills/positioning.md`. Every other GTM skill reads this
file first. The lines below are Search's own starting point; a fork
should rewrite them for the people in `local/profile.md`.

- **Who it's for:** people whose work is spread over a CRM, a mailbox, a
  calendar, docs, an issue tracker and chat, and who lose time finding
  the one thing someone said.
- **The problem:** every tool has its own search box, and none of them
  knows about the others.
- **The promise:** one search box for everything you connect, with an AI
  that answers from what it found and cites it.
- **The proof:** on the Acme Inc sample, "globex renewal" finds the
  HubSpot deal, the Gmail thread, the signature call and the Outlook
  redline in one list, and "Where are we with Globex?" answers
  in three sentences with three citations.

Until a fork rewrites these, GTM skills should ask before reusing them.
